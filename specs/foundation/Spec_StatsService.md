# This spec has been superseded

The Stats Service has been extracted into a standalone project: `nfl-stats-service`.

- **Standalone service spec:** `nfl-stats-service/docs/Spec_NflStatsService.md`
- **XO Play consumer spec:** `specs/foundation/Spec_StatsServiceConsumer.md` (this folder)

The Stats Service is no longer part of XO Play's codebase. XO Play is a consumer of the shared NFL Stats Service, which also serves Apex and future sports products.

**Decision date:** May 2026
